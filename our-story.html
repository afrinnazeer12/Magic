The Heart Behind Magic Delightz

Magic Delightz started in 2021 with only a few clients, but with dedication, consistency, and homemade quality, it gradually reached many hearts and many celebrations.

Founded by Nisha Nazeer, our CEO, Magic Delightz was created with a true passion for baking and a strong commitment to making every cake special, fresh, and memorable.

Our Co-Founder, Abdul Nazeer, has also been an important part of this journey, helping grow the brand with care, support, and vision.

We proudly prepare our cakes with pure ingredients, homemade care, and honest quality. We also make sure our pricing remains fair, so our customers receive beautiful, delicious cakes at great value.
